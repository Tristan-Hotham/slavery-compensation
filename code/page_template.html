Where slavery money came home

{{q1.gb|int}} addresses in Great Britain linked to people recorded as slave-owners when slavery was abolished in the 1830s (Legacies of British Slavery, UCL). Parliament paid £20 million to the owners. The enslaved people received nothing. Tap a dot or a county; search a town.

{{BYLINE}}

    Layers

    Mapped: the {{flow.gb|int}} geocoded addresses in Great Britain, plus {{flow.ireland|int}} in Ireland and {{flow.channel|int}} in the Channel Islands (grey). England and Wales counties are approximate outlines rebuilt from 1851 parishes; Scotland's counties are circles. About {{flow.lbs_table_located_pc|f0}}% of the addresses in the LBS county table have been located; the rest are not on the map.
